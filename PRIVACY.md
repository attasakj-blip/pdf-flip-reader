# Privacy Policy for Pagelume

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Pagelume ("the app") is made by Attasak (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Your files stay on your phone
- The PDF files you open are copied into the app's own storage on your phone so you can reopen them. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- Your settings (language, page sounds, night mode, recent books and the page you stopped at) are saved only on your phone.
- The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
- Translation runs on your phone with Google ML Kit. The text you translate is not sent anywhere. The first time you translate into or from a language, the app downloads that language pack (about 30 MB) from Google's servers.
- Signatures, edited files and images you copy stay on your phone, in the places you choose.

## Advertising
The app is free and shows a small banner ad on the file screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:
- the advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- information about ad views and clicks, and diagnostic information.

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences. How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
